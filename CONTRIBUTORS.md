## How to be listed

1. Get a pull request merged. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [AI policy](AI_POLICY.md).
2. Add a row (or extend yours) in a PR: name, GitHub handle, and a one-line summary of what you changed, with issue/PR numbers when available.
3. Keep the table ordering rule above. Do not add people who have not contributed, and do not list AI tools as contributors.
